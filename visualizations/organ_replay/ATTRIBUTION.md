## Original illustrative geometry

Tumor tissue and cutaway tumor-cell meshes are generated by `morphology.js`.
They are generic morphology illustrations with fixed geometry, not sample
reconstructions, microscopy measurements, or predictions of spatial uptake.
Surface texture and generic organelle details use fixed display-only random seeds.
No patient image, microscopy reconstruction or third-party cell mesh was used.
The tissue cut surface, cell membrane, nucleus and organelles are explanatory
illustrations; colors are illustrative rather than calibrated microscopy colors.
Anatomical display details have no effect on the quantitative time series.
The current soft cyan quantity wash is an original display treatment implemented
in `amount_signal.js`. Geometry and base material properties are fixed; a uniform
amount parameter controls the wash while facing/lighting preserves depth cues.
Coloring does not represent measured tissue staining, nuclear uptake or a
predicted spatial drug distribution. The rejected v5 section/fill implementation
is no longer part of the active viewer.
