# Historical study protocol: release methods and connection to CABOMB

This file preserves the protocol for the completed local study. Its execution
gate, workstation environment and installation/upload restrictions describe
that historical run. Current package commands and reproduction scope are in
`../REPRODUCIBILITY.md` and `../../docs/run_guide.md`.

This is method development and a retrospective comparison. Every late curve has been seen previously. A freeze governs this execution round; it does not restore blinding. User scope includes both public-series forecasting and an explicit connection to the original CABOMB release submodule. No source file, original model, historical output, input, or raw data is overwritten. No install, upload, or commit is authorized.

## A. Statistical comparison

The primary calcium example is the two published pH curves in DOI [10.1038/s41467-024-54402-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-54402-y), Figure 1g and source workbook Figure1 C185:G200. The workbook says DCNP; the figure caption says DCNP-Ab and the text says DCNP. These are coated calcium nanoparticles, not CABOMB OCN/YSA carriers. The main text describes an assay for total calcium after acid digestion, but the calcium-release separation, sampling and exact percentage denominator are not established by the inspected main text. The author-reported percentage is retained without inventing those details. The fitted plateau A is a statistical coefficient, not an experimentally established maximum releasable fraction.

Calcium training is t=1,2,4,6,8,12 h; late evaluation is 24,36,48,72,96 h. The three auxiliary external studies keep their original early and late splits, 12 curves total. The combined panel has 14 curves from four studies, with one calcium study and three cargo-release studies. Same-study and related formulations are dependent. No replicate trajectories are invented from means or SD, and no curve or study is removed after results. Structural zeros are shown but excluded from objective and sample counts.

All new comparisons use one pipeline: x=t/T, with T the full-early final time and held fixed inside rolling fits, percentage-scale unweighted SSE, 33 deterministic starts and common solver tolerances. The exact configuration is `frozen_config.json`. The four original families are re-fitted with the external-validation pipeline's bounds. The calcium pilot used different physical-time bounds/transforms; its historical result is reference context only and cannot be credited as the baseline improvement of a new family.

The parameter order for each family is explicit in `parameter_order` in the configuration. HC uses the same positive `k_x` bounds [1e-8,1000] and logarithmic transform as exp100. A fit is numerically unresolved if it has no successful finite start, or if any unsuccessful start has RMSE more than 1e-6 pp below the successful optimum. Every terminal optimizer result remains available. Recomputed old4SSE predictions for the 12 external curves must agree with their frozen historical predictions to max absolute error <=1e-6 pp. If not, report a pipeline-parity failure and do not attribute changed baseline performance to new methods.

Write F in percent. Original four families: `exp100=100(1-exp(-kx*x))`; `expA=A(1-exp(-kx*x))`; `weibull100=100(1-exp(-(x/taux)^beta))`; `capped_linear=min(100,sx*x)`. New families:

| WeibullA | F=A[1-exp(-t^beta/alpha)], DDSolver Table I #330 | A[1-exp(-(x/taux)^beta)], alpha=tau^beta | A,taux,beta |
| HiguchiCap | F=kH*t^(1/2), #308 | min(100,kHx*x^(1/2)) | kHx |

[DDSolver (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1208/s12248-010-9185-1) supplies model-library forms, not evidence that they predict unseen late behavior. The three caps are analyst extensions outside the original formula's applicable regime. Higuchi and KP are primarily early-release descriptions with material/geometry assumptions. KP is included here as a deliberately empirical, domain-stress benchmark fitted to all early points. A reported percentage below 60% of nominal initial amount would not by itself establish F/F_infinity<=0.6, and the calcium denominator is unresolved. No fitted exponent diagnoses diffusion, erosion or a CABOMB mechanism. Hopfenberg is omitted to keep the library small; the cube-root family is already represented by HC. No lag, burst intercept, mixture fit or more-than-three-parameter candidate is added.

Two naive methods are fixed in advance. Hold-last returns the final observed early percentage, capped to [0,100]. Last3OLS-slope anchored-last fits an OLS slope to the final three positive early points, replaces a negative slope with zero, anchors the forecast at the last observed value and time, and caps to [0,100]. It does not use the fitted OLS intercept. These are analyst baselines.

All eight single fitted families and both naive methods are reported. Three selectors are distinct: (1) old4SSE chooses the smallest full-early RMSE among the four old families; (2) old4Rolling changes only selection to rolling prediction loss; (3) expandedRolling uses eight families plus two naive methods. Rolling uses prefixes with at least four positive times and predicts the next early point. All candidates face the same folds; score is sqrt(mean squared fold errors), equally weighting the common target times. At least two folds are required. Any candidate with a failed/nonfinite/unresolved fold is ineligible rather than having its failed fold dropped. If there are fewer than two folds or no eligible candidate with a usable full-early fit, fall back to old4SSE and label it as fallback. If a ranked candidate has a failed full fit, skip to the next eligible rank and record that skip. If old4SSE itself cannot be computed, output unresolved and null predictions; do not convert failure into success. Exact ties follow listed method order within 1e-8 pp. Late values never enter fitting, CV, rank, eligibility or fallback.

The primary error summary is mean late RMSE within each study and then equal weighting of the four studies. Every curve and study remains visible, with RMSE>10 pp failure counts, terminal signed/absolute errors, maximum worsening relative to same-pipeline old4SSE and unscorable cases. Missing results do not silently disappear from aggregate denominators: aggregates are marked incomplete when any constituent is unscorable. A smaller study-equal error is descriptive improvement on this known panel, not confirmation. Single families, selectors, naive forecasts, successful and failed optimizations, boundary solutions, Jacobian rank/singular values and nominal n-p are all retained. No SE, p-value, AIC or universal accuracy claim is appropriate here.

## B. Bridge to the existing CABOMB model

The source V1.3 module uses `J_deg=k_deg(pH)*A_internalized` and `dA_cargo/dt=Y_cargo*J_deg-k_cargo_loss*A_cargo`. `k_deg(pH)` is an assumed piecewise linear interpolation between acidic and physiological endpoints. The registry marks its numerical rates as assumed exploratory. A synchronized internalized batch at fixed pH has an exponential cumulative release kernel under this formulation. That is the mathematical link to A, not a biological validation from the auxiliary curves.

Run the unchanged original V1.3 RHS, explicit circulation, 200 nm, medium EPR/MPS and default scenario (intracellular pH 5.5, distinct from the calcium study acidic pH 5.0), four designs, 0–72 h. Record the complete actual parameter vector and mode/scenario values, including intracellular pH. Record the source hashes. Extract the two internalization fluxes and sum them to form J(t). These fluxes depend on extracellular/bound states, not on internalized carrier; the fixed-input postprocessing does not modify ODE dynamics.

For an age a measured since internalization, assume `F(a)=1-exp(-(a/tau)^beta)`. Use beta={0.5,1,2}, plateau 1, and the same mean waiting time 4 h, so tau=4/Gamma(1+1/beta). The 4 h mean matches 1/0.25 h^-1 only as a transparent reference to the original assumed acidic rate. All shape values and their transfer to an internalized-carrier-equivalent response are explicit analyst assumptions. No calcium-fitted parameter, calcium percentage, Y_cargo value or cargo yield is transferred.

Compute `Q(t)=integral_0^t J(s) F(t-s) ds`, `M(t)=integral_0^t J(s) ds`, and `P(t)=M(t)-Q(t)`. Q is gross kernel response; P is pending response. Q+P=M is a separate ledger in normalized internalized-carrier-equivalent units. P is not the original A_internalized; Q is neither measured cargo nor free intracellular calcium. Do not sum this ledger into the carrier ledger, and do not label Q as active A_cargo remaining after loss. Each arriving cohort has its own age; injection-clock hazards must never be applied to all cohorts.

Use nonnegative trapezoidal bin masses placed at interval midpoints and multiply each cohort mass by F(age). Solve the original RHS with dense output and evaluate its fluxes at the required grid endpoints; report any numerical negative values and only zero numerical values with magnitude <=1e-10. Compare grids delta={.05,.025,.0125} h; the maximum gross-response difference between the last two grids must be <=1e-4 normalized units or remain unresolved without claiming convergence. Show the exact ledger residual at every grid and save the full curves and report 4/12/24/48/72 h response and design ordering for all three fixed kernels, irrespective of whether ordering changes.

Only negative fluxes in [-1e-10,0) may be clipped to zero, with count and minimum recorded. Small positive fluxes are preserved. More negative fluxes fail the check. For beta=1, require max absolute Q minus (M_solver minus original A_internalized) <=1e-4 normalized units; here M_solver is an extra cumulative-J quadrature state integrated with the unchanged original RHS. Also require trapezoid cumulative input M versus M_solver <=1e-4, plus the three-grid convergence check; this distinguishes original ODE trajectory error and age quadrature error. Record solver tolerances and results separately. Initial internalized amount is zero in this fixed scenario.

## Verification and execution gate

Before real fits or B simulations, obtain independent scope PASS on these files. Test mathematical bounds/zero/monotonicity; synthetic recovery; deterministic starts; late-value isolation; rolling fold construction and failure fallback; Naive anchoring; original-four same-pipeline parity; parameter metadata; mass ledger/nonnegativity; impulse/constant input against analytic exponential convolution; first-order parity against original model internalized+degraded identity; and quadrature convergence. Keep all tests local and write only this directory, with bytecode disabled and plotting caches directed here. Runtime is the existing repository `.venv` without installation. Inputs are derived copies with source paths, hashes and cell mappings; originals remain intact. Every empirical coefficient bound, units, status, source and every bridge assumption must appear in the local registry. Source uncertainty and failed checks remain visible in STATUS and report.
